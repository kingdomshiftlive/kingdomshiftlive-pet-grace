## Month 1–3: Build Traffic

**Content:**
- Publish 2 new blog posts per month targeting Google keywords
- Target keywords: "pet loss grief support," "dog quality of life assessment," "how to cope with losing a pet"
- These are high-intent, low-competition keywords in an underserved niche

**Community:**
- Share in pet loss Facebook groups (search "pet loss support" — thousands of active groups)
- Share in r/dogs, r/cats, r/petloss on Reddit
- Reach out to pet loss counselors and veterinarians for backlinks

**Email:**
- Offer a free "Pet Loss Grief Guide" PDF in exchange for email signup
- Send weekly pet care tips to build list engagement

**Estimated Month 3 revenue at 5,000 visitors/mo:** $150–$475

## The Pet Loss Niche Advantage

Pet loss grief is one of the most underserved emotional niches online. People who have lost a pet:
- Search desperately for support and information
- Are highly receptive to compassionate, faith-based content
- Have high purchase intent for memorial products and support resources
- Return to sites that made them feel understood

The Quality of Life Assessment tool is a particularly strong traffic magnet — pet owners facing end-of-life decisions search for this type of tool constantly. No major competitor has built one with this level of care and detail.

This is a niche with real emotional resonance and real revenue potential.
